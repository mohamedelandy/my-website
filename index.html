About Mohamed
Architecting high-scale apps
and polished native tools

I'm a Senior React Native Developer based in Cairo, Egypt, with over 8 years of production experience building scalable, clean-architecture mobile systems across e-commerce, real estate, on-demand services, and open source libraries.

My career has revolved around engineering quality: writing type-safe TypeScript, designing custom native iOS/Android bridges, eliminating jank with Reanimated 4 and Skia, and establishing automated CI/CD pipelines (Azure DevOps, GitHub Actions, Maestro E2E testing) so shipping feels effortless.

I'm passionate about the open source ecosystem — having authored and published react-native-foldface on npm to deliver fluid 3D fold transitions powered by Reanimated worklets.

Whether building multi-store architectures serving tens of thousands of shoppers or crafting native iOS widgets in Swift, I take end-to-end technical ownership from architectural blueprints to App Store deployment.
